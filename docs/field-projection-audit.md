# FIELD-000 field projection audit

This is the FIELD-000 docs-only audit for per-field privilege projection in Coronatio. It creates no source, Cargo, or wall changes. Its job is to map every guest-reachable fact route, classify mixed fields by the five fact-kinds, and name the ruling table that lets delete-by-denylist die.

## A. Method and governing law recap

Method: read the live Coronatio route table and handlers at this worktree head; read the already-admitted stats/pulse audit documents; read the Paligenesis governing leaves; then classify only facts that are served to guest-reachable surfaces. Mutation-only routes are listed where they return fact readbacks or are in the operator-provided route census; `/api/admin/*` is treated as one whole-route admin class.

Governing law: the true-form leaf defines the five fact-kinds (`identity`, `lifecycle`, `privilege`, `preference`, `secret`) and requires one session membrane with construction under policy, not filtration after construction (`/fulcrum/attachments/paligenesis/docs/workflow/coronatio-eternal-appliance-true-form-law.md:62-77`, `/fulcrum/attachments/paligenesis/docs/workflow/coronatio-eternal-appliance-true-form-law.md:91-100`). The pulse leaf says pokes carry nothing and every read is a session-projected pull (`/fulcrum/attachments/paligenesis/docs/workflow/workflow-coronatio-pulse-organ-poke-substrate-law.md:26-44`, `/fulcrum/attachments/paligenesis/docs/workflow/workflow-coronatio-pulse-organ-poke-substrate-law.md:74-82`). The stats route is already lawful: `/api/stats` chooses `project_system_stats_admin()` or `project_system_stats_guest()` from the session membrane (`src/bands/routes.rs:87-93`), and the guest type cannot represent denied fields (`src/bands/contracts/core.rs:31-42`, `src/bands/contracts/core.rs:109-145`).
